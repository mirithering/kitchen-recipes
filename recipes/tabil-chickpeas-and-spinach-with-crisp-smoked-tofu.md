# Tabil Chickpeas and Spinach with Crisp Smoked Tofu

**Time:** 25 min active / 30 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal | Step |
|------------|--------|--------|------|------|
| Caraway seeds              |   2g | 1 tsp     |   7 | 1 |
| Cumin seeds                |   2g | 1 tsp     |   8 | 1 |
| Coriander seeds            |   3g | 1 3/4 tsp |   9 | 1 |
| Olive oil                  |   9g | 2 tsp     |  80 | 2 |
| Onion                      |  70g | 1/2 cup   |  28 | 2 |
| Garlic                     |   9g | 3 cloves  |  13 | 2 |
| Sweet paprika              |   2g | 1 tsp     |   6 | 2 |
| Cayenne pepper             | 0.5g | 1/4 tsp   |   2 | 2 |
| Canned chickpeas           | 125g | 1/2 can   | 172 | 3 |
| Cherry tomatoes            |  85g | 5 cherry  |  15 | 3 |
| Water                      |  80g | 1/3 cup   |   0 | 3 |
| Almond sesame smoked tofu  | 100g | 1/2 block |  98 | 4 |
| Fresh spinach              | 120g | 4 handfuls|  28 | 5 |
| Lemon juice                |  15g | 1 tbsp    |   3 | 6 |
| Olive oil                  |   5g | 1 tsp     |  44 | 6 |

**Total:** 513 kcal | 26g protein

## Steps

1. Toast the caraway, cumin, and coriander seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, swirling often, for about 2-3 minutes until fragrant and one shade darker; tip into a mortar and grind to a coarse powder.
2. Warm the cooking olive oil in the same pan over medium heat and fry the onion, stirring often, for about 8 minutes until soft and golden at the edges. Stir in the garlic, ground tabil, paprika, and cayenne and toast for about 1 minute until the spices smell nutty.
3. Add the chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, and water and bring to a gentle simmer. Mash about half the chickpeas against the side of the pan with a fork, then simmer, stirring now and then, for about 12-15 minutes until thick and creamy and the tomatoes have burst.
4. Meanwhile, cut the smoked tofu into bite-size cubes and crisp them in a dry nonstick pan over medium heat, turning, for about 6-7 minutes until golden on several sides -- watch closely so the almond-sesame coating browns but never scorches.
5. Stir the spinach into the chickpeas a handful at a time and cook for just 1-2 minutes, only until wilted and still bright green; taste and season with salt.
6. Spoon the chickpeas into a bowl, pile the crisp tofu on top, finish with the lemon juice and a thread of cold olive oil.

## Notes

Tabil is Tunisia's everyday spice blend -- coriander leads and caraway is its signature backbone (the word once meant coriander itself). Toasting the seeds whole before grinding wakes their oils and rounds off any raw, soapy edge, and grinding them fresh keeps the blend far brighter than pre-ground powder. Mashing half the chickpeas is the trick to a creamy braise without cream or baking soda: their starch thickens the liquid while the whole ones keep their bite. Spinach goes in at the very end and only just wilts -- cook it longer and it weeps water and turns slippery. The tofu stays on top rather than in the sauce so its coating keeps its crunch; left to sit in liquid it softens within minutes. The thread of cold, raw olive oil over the finished bowl is the Tunisian move that lifts an earthy legume dish.
