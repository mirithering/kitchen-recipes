# Rose-Raspberry Semolina Porridge with Toasted Seeds

**Time:** 12 min active / 12 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal |
| ------------ | -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Pumpkin seeds | 12g | 1 1/2 tbsp | 67 |
| Soy milk | 250g | 1 cup | 102 |
| Semolina | 45g | 1/4 cup | 162 |
| Frozen raspberries | 80g | 2/3 cup | 42 |
| Honey | 12g | 1 3/4 tsp | 36 |
| Egg | 50g | 3 1/2 tbsp | 72 |
| Hemp seeds | 10g | 3 tsp | 55 |

**Total:** 536 kcal | 27g protein

## Steps

1. Dry-toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry saucepan over medium heat, shaking often, for about 2 minutes until they puff and smell nutty; tip out and reserve. Hemp seeds stay raw.
2. In the same pan bring the soy milk and a pinch of salt to a gentle boil, then rain in the semolina while whisking constantly to keep it lump-free. Drop to the lowest heat and stir like risotto for about 8 minutes until thick and creamy.
3. Meanwhile warm the raspberries with the honey in a small pan over medium heat, just until they slump and release their juice but still hold their shape -- 2 to 3 minutes, not jammy. Off the heat, stir in a small splash of rose water.
4. Take the porridge off the heat. Whisk the egg in a bowl, drizzle in a few spoonfuls of the hot porridge while whisking to temper it, then stir it back into the pot off the heat for a custardy finish.
5. Spoon the porridge into a bowl, ripple the warm raspberries through the top, and scatter over the toasted pumpkin and raw hemp seeds.

## Notes

Semolina rained into near-boiling liquid while whisking hydrates evenly and stays smooth -- adding it to cold milk or stopping the whisk is what makes it lumpy. Tempering the egg off the heat (hot porridge into cold egg, never the reverse) keeps it below the ~80C where it would scramble, so it thickens to silk instead of curdling. Cooking the frozen raspberries only briefly keeps the pectin intact and the flavor bright and tart -- a long simmer with more sugar turns them to jam and dulls them. Rose is a classic partner to raspberry; a small splash lifts the fruit without turning it perfumed.
