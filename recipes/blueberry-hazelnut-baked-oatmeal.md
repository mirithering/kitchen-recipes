# Blueberry-Hazelnut Baked Oatmeal

**Time:** 10 min active / 35 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal | Step |
|------------|--------|--------|------|------|
| Hazelnuts          | 15g | 2 tbsp chopped | 94 | 1 |
| Egg                | 50g | 1 medium       | 72 | 2 |
| Soy Milk           | 150g | 1/2 cup       | 62 | 2 |
| Almond Butter      | 15g | 1 tbsp         | 92 | 2 |
| Maple Syrup        | 15g | 3/4 tbsp       | 39 | 2 |
| Vanilla Extract    | 2g  | 1/2 tsp        |  6 | 2 |
| Orange Zest        | 2g  | 1/2 tsp        |  0 | 2 |
| Salt               | 1g  | pinch          |  0 | 2 |
| Rolled Oats        | 50g | 2/3 cup        | 190 | 3 |
| Hemp Seeds         | 15g | 1 1/2 tbsp     | 83 | 3 |
| Frozen Blueberries | 80g | 1/2 cup        | 41 | 3 |
| Rapeseed Oil       | 2g  | 1/2 tsp        | 18 | 4 |

**Total:** 697 kcal | 27g protein

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 180C. Spread the hazelnuts on a small tray and toast for 8-10 minutes, until the skins crack and the centers smell deeply roasted; tip them onto a clean towel, rub hard to shed most of the skins, then chop coarsely.
2. In a bowl, whisk the egg, soy milk, almond butter, maple syrup, vanilla, orange zest, and salt until completely smooth -- the almond butter must fully disperse or it sinks into a pool.
3. Stir in the oats and hemp seeds and let stand 5 minutes so the oats drink in the custard. Fold in the blueberries straight from the freezer with a few light turns -- frozen and barely stirred, they hold their shape instead of bleeding gray.
4. Brush a small ramekin or baking dish with the oil, scrape in the batter, and scatter the toasted hazelnuts over the top. Bake 22-25 minutes, until the edges are puffed and set but the center still wobbles softly when nudged; pull it there. It firms as it cools -- a clean-baked center means it has gone rubbery.

## Notes

Baked oatmeal is really a loose oat custard: the egg networks across the milk to set it, so the texture lives or dies on the bake. Stop while the middle jiggles -- carryover heat and cooling carry it the rest of the way, and an extra five minutes is the difference between creamy and dry.

Hazelnut works in two registers here -- toasted nuts on top for crunch and roast, almond butter blended in for a smooth nutty backbone -- while orange zest and vanilla lift the blueberry: the zest's citrus oils echo the floral top note that vanilla rounds out. Frozen berries are an asset, not a compromise; their cold slows the bleed and keeps them whole.
