# Tofu Kho -- Vietnamese Caramel-Braised Tofu with Mushrooms

**Time:** 30 min active / 50 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal |
| --------------- | ------ | ---------- | ---- |
| Firm Tofu | 200g | 1 block | 170 |
| White Rice | 65g | 1/3 cup | 234 |
| Rapeseed Oil | 10g | 2 1/4 tsp | 88 |
| Sugar | 15g | 3 1/2 tsp | 58 |
| Water | 120g | 1/2 cup | 0 |
| White Onion | 60g | 1/2 medium | 24 |
| Ginger | 10g | 1 1/2 tbsp | 8 |
| Coconut Milk | 30g | 2 tbsp | 59 |
| Fish Sauce | 15g | 2 1/2 tsp | 5 |
| Soy Sauce | 8g | 1 1/2 tsp | 4 |
| Fresh Mushrooms | 100g | 1 1/2 cup | 22 |
| Dried Chili | 1g | pinch | 3 |
| Black Pepper | 1g | pinch | 3 |
| Fresh Spinach | 80g | 2 1/2 cup | 18 |

**Total:** 696 kcal \| 35g protein

## Steps

1. Cut the tofu into thick slabs and press between clean towels under a weighted board or pan for about 15 minutes, until the towels are damp and the tofu feels firm and dry to the touch.
2. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook covered until tender, about 12-15 minutes, and leave it lidded to steam.
3. Pat the pressed tofu bone-dry on every side and cut into bite-size cubes. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat in a single layer, turning each face only once after it has set a golden skin, about 2-3 minutes a side; flipping too early tears the crust. Lift out and set aside.
4. In the same pan, melt the sugar over medium heat without stirring, swirling the pan, until it turns deep amber and smells toasty and nutty, about 4-6 minutes. Pull it off the heat the instant the smell sharpens -- burnt caramel goes bitter and cannot be rescued.
5. Stir the onion and ginger into the caramel and cook about 1 minute until fragrant, then carefully pour in the water, coconut milk, fish sauce and soy sauce; it will splutter and the caramel may seize, then melt smooth as it heats.
6. Return the tofu with the mushrooms and crumbled dried chili. Simmer uncovered, turning the tofu now and then, for about 15-20 minutes, until the sauce reduces to a glossy mahogany lacquer that clings to every piece. Finish with a generous crack of black pepper.
7. Wilt the spinach in a hot dry pan for about 1 minute until just collapsed but still bright green. Serve the tofu and its lacquer over the rice with the spinach alongside.

## Notes

Kho means to simmer in a salty-sweet caramel until the liquid all but vanishes and lacquers what is left -- a southern Vietnamese home technique. The caramel (nuoc mau) carries the whole dish: take the sugar only to deep amber and pull it the moment it smells toasty, because the bitterness of burnt sugar is permanent and no amount of braising will lift it. Pressing and dry-frying the tofu first builds a skin that drinks the sauce without falling apart, while the mushrooms throw their own umami into the glaze. Coconut milk rounds the salt of the fish sauce, and the black pepper added at the end is traditional and meant to be assertive -- don't be shy with it.
