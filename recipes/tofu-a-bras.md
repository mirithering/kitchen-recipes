# Tofu a Bras

**Time:** 20 min active / 30 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal |
| ------------ | -------- | ------------- | ------ |
| Firm Tofu | 150g | 3/4 block | 128 |
| Sweet Potato | 150g | 1 cup | 129 |
| Olive Oil | 5g | 1 tsp | 44 |
| White Onion | 120g | 3/4 medium | 48 |
| Olive Oil | 9g | 2 tsp | 80 |
| Garlic | 9g | 3 cloves | 13 |
| Egg | 100g | 2 large | 143 |
| Black Olives | 20g | 4-5 large | 23 |
| Dried Parsley | 1g | 2 tsp | 3 |
| Lemon | 10g | 1 wedge | 3 |

**Total:** 614 kcal | 34g protein

## Steps

1. Wrap the tofu in a clean towel, sit a heavy pan on top, and press for about 15 minutes until it stops weeping water.
2. Heat the oven to 220C. Cut the sweet potato into thin matchstick batons, toss with olive oil and salt, spread in a single uncrowded layer, and roast for 22-25 minutes, turning once, until the edges are deep golden and crisp.
3. Slice the onion thin. Fry in olive oil over medium heat, stirring often, for 8-10 minutes until soft and golden; add the sliced garlic for the last minute until fragrant.
4. Tear the pressed tofu into ragged bite-size shards. Push the onion to one side, lay the tofu in a single layer, and fry undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until the undersides are golden and release cleanly, then turn to crisp the other side. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper. Fold the roasted sweet potato through the tofu and onion so everything is evenly tangled.
6. Take the pan OFF the heat, pour in the eggs, and fold gently for 30-60 seconds; the residual heat sets them into a glossy, barely-set custard. Slide onto the plate while still soft and creamy -- never dry.
7. Scatter with chopped olives and parsley, add a squeeze of lemon, and finish with black pepper.

## Notes

Bacalhau a Bras is Lisbon comfort food -- salt cod tangled with onions, crisp potato straws, and eggs barely set into a creamy bind. Here pressed firm tofu stands in for the cod: pressing drives out water so the torn shards crisp at the edges and drink up the egg the way flaked fish does. The egg is everything. Pull the pan fully off the heat before it goes in -- egg protein sets at 63-70C, and a hot pan turns it to dry, weeping curds within seconds. Folded into residual warmth it stays glossy and custardy; plate it while it still looks a touch underdone, because carryover heat finishes the set on the way to the table. A home oven can't crisp true potato straws -- at matchstick thinness they only steam -- so sweet potato batons roasted hot give caramelized, craggy edges instead, a little sweetness playing against the briny olives and bright lemon.
