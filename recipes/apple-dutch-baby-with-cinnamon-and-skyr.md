# Apple Dutch Baby with Cinnamon and Skyr

**Time:** 15 min active / 40 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal |
| ------------ | -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Egg | 100g | 2 large | 143 |
| Soy Milk | 90g | 1/3 cup | 37 |
| Spelt Wholemeal Flour | 40g | 1/3 cup | 135 |
| Vanilla Extract | 2g | 1/2 tsp | 6 |
| Ground Cinnamon | 0.5g | 1/4 tsp | 1 |
| Nutmeg | 0.2g | pinch | 1 |
| Rapeseed Oil | 8g | 1 3/4 tsp | 71 |
| Green Apple | 128g | 3/4 medium | 67 |
| Maple Syrup | 15g | 2 1/4 tsp | 39 |
| Skyr | 80g | 1/3 cup | 41 |

**Total:** 541 kcal | 29g protein

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 220C with a rack in the upper third, and slide an oven-safe skillet (cast iron or steel) in to preheat with it -- a screaming-hot pan is what makes the batter climb the walls.
2. Blend the eggs, soy milk, flour, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a pinch of salt for 20-30 seconds until smooth and frothy with bubbles on top. Let it rest at room temperature for 15 minutes -- this hydrates the wholemeal spelt so it puffs instead of sitting dense.
3. Pull the hot skillet onto the stovetop over medium-high, add the oil, then the apple slices in a single layer. Drizzle over the maple and cook, turning once, for 4-5 minutes until golden and just tender and the juices go syrupy.
4. Give the rested batter one last whisk and pour it over the apples. Slide straight into the oven and bake 18-22 minutes until dramatically puffed, deep golden, and crisp at the edges. Do NOT open the door before 18 minutes or it will collapse.
5. Dollop the cold skyr over the hot pancake and serve immediately, before it deflates.

## Notes

A Dutch baby is a popover you bake in a pan: the thin, egg-heavy batter hits a blazing surface, the water flashes to steam, and the egg proteins set around the expanding bubbles before they can escape -- so it billows up the sides. Three things protect that rise: room-temperature batter (cold batter makes lazy steam), blending rather than stirring (over-worked gluten turns it rubbery), and an unopened oven door for the first 18 minutes.

Wholemeal spelt has bran that cuts the gluten strands, so it bakes a touch denser than white flour -- the 15-minute rest and a high oven win most of that puff back. Searing the apples first is not optional: raw slices weep into the batter and leave a soggy, sunken middle, while caramelizing drives off that surface water and trades it for browned-sugar depth. The cold tang of skyr against the hot sweet pancake is the whole point -- no sugar needed on top.
