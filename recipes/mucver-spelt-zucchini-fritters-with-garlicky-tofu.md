# Mucver -- Spelt Zucchini Fritters with Garlicky Tofu

**Time:** 20 min active / 30 min total

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume | Kcal | Step |
|------------|--------|--------|------|------|
| Zucchini       | 200g | 1 medium         |  34 | 1 |
| Garlic         |   3g | 1 clove          |   4 | 2 |
| Lemon Juice    |  10g | 2 tsp            |   2 | 2 |
| Silken Tofu    |  84g | 1 slice          |  52 | 2 |
| Egg            |  50g | 1 egg            |  72 | 4 |
| Spelt Flour    |  30g | 3 1/2 tbsp       | 101 | 4 |
| Swiss Cheese   |  35g | 1/3 cup shredded | 138 | 4 |
| Shallot        |  30g | 3 tbsp           |  22 | 4 |
| Dried Dill     |   1g | 1 tsp            |   3 | 4 |
| Olive Oil      |  14g | 1 tbsp           | 124 | 5 |

**Total:** 552 kcal | 29g protein

## Steps

1. Coarsely grate the zucchini, toss it with a good pinch of salt, and leave it to drain in a colander while you make the sauce -- at least 15 minutes. The salt pulls the water out so the fritters crisp instead of steam.
2. Stir the grated garlic into the lemon juice and leave it to sit the same 15 minutes -- the acid takes off its raw bite. Blend it with the silken tofu and a pinch of salt until smooth and creamy, then chill.
3. Squeeze the drained zucchini very firmly, a handful at a time, until no more liquid runs and it feels dry to the touch. This is the difference between crisp and soggy.
4. Mix the squeezed zucchini with the egg, spelt flour, shredded cheese, finely chopped shallot, dill, and black pepper into a thick batter that holds its shape on the spoon -- add no extra liquid.
5. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Drop in heaped spoonfuls, flatten each to a finger's thickness, and fry undisturbed 3-4 minutes until the underside is deep golden and firm before you flip; fry the second side until crisp.
6. Serve the fritters hot with the cold garlicky tofu sauce alongside.

## Notes

Mucver is the Turkish answer to a glut of summer squash, and its whole secret is moisture: grated zucchini is up to 95% water, so salting and then squeezing it bone-dry is non-negotiable -- skip it and the batter loosens and the fritters steam pale in the pan. One egg is enough binder here because the wholemeal spelt, with its thirsty bran, soaks up what little moisture remains; resist thinning the batter. Fry at a steady medium heat and flip only once the first side is set, or the crust tears before the inside cooks. Traditionally these come with garlicky yogurt; with none in the house, whipped silken tofu stands in beautifully -- blending it aerates it to a cool, tangy cream, and steeping the garlic in lemon first tames its sharpness so the sauce stays bright, not hot.
